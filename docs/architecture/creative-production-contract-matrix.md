## PRODUCT_IDENTITY

No model answer: resolveProductIdentity(brief, bytes): the product is a pinned asset or the state is explicitly MISSING, AMBIGUOUS or UNSUPPORTED

## SUBSTITUTE_DETECTION

No model answer: detectSubstitutes(text, identity): the lexicon of the Brain, the family of the product and the words that name it

## CAPABILITY_ROUTING

No model answer: selectTool(capability, constraints): ranked candidates declared, one chosen and recorded, none used as a fallback

## SCENE_SPEC

No model answer: buildSceneSpec(...): the layers, their z-order, their requests, their provenance; a scene that cannot be specified is BLOCKED or INTENT_ONLY

## TEXT_INTEGRITY

No model answer: textLayersOf, checkTextFeasibility and recoverLockedCopy: the copy is never changed to fit and is recoverable byte for byte

## PRODUCTION_COST

No model answer: estimateProduction and reserveProduction: every line reserved before it is incurred against a hard ceiling

## PRODUCTION_STATE

No model answer: the production planner: nodes stored with the hash of their inputs, reused only when it is unchanged

## LAYER_COMPOSITOR

No model answer: composeLayers: pixel-exact z-ordered composition of the raster layers with measured, bounded overlap

## PROP_MATTE

No model answer: matteProp: a generated prop is cut out of its plain ground or refused with the measurement that failed

## LAYERED_RUNTIME

No model answer: runLayeredCreative: a specified scene is executed end to end through the gates of the single-image runtime; only the selected tool runs; a stop names its replanning

## PROVIDER_GATE

No model answer: evaluateProviderGate: fails closed; never calls a provider

PARITY: every production rule is enforced by a validator that names it, and every probe holds.
