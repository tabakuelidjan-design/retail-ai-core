# HABB — internal business principles (merchant-specific, DRAFT for owner review)

Merchant configuration, not architecture: nothing in the generic core reads or depends on this file. It expresses how HABB wants to be run, on top of the generic principles in `docs/principles/decision-principles.md`. It contains only facts already established in the HABB project; the owner should correct or extend it — it is a draft, not a decision.

Like the generic principles, **it never changes a financial calculation**; it will only shape how future recommendations are framed and filtered.

## Who HABB is

Personalised gifts and UV printing, physical shop in Namur (Belgium) plus habb.be online. Real customers, live production store.

## Working principles (from the owner's stated direction)

1. **Premium, sober, credible.** Growth ideas must fit a restrained premium brand — no generic "AI-looking" or gimmicky promotion, no fake urgency, no decorative excess.
2. **Never invent facts.** Prices, specs, compatibility and claims must be verified against the real catalogue before being stated.
3. **Cash is limited, so evidence comes first.** Runway is limited: prefer actions that improve cash and margin quickly and reversibly; be sceptical of stock or spend whose economics cannot be shown. Costs that are unverified are treated as unverified, not as good news.
4. **Local acquisition matters.** Foot traffic and local visibility in Namur are a stated priority over more site development.
5. **Target real buyers directly.** Prefer reaching the actual gift buyers (e.g. parents) over indirect partnerships that do not fit HABB's Belgian, customised model (owner decided against school partnerships).
6. **Protect what already works.** The live shop is production: changes are staged and approved by the owner before anything customer-visible happens.
7. **Use tools already paid for** (Canva, ChatGPT, CapCut, Kling, Claude) before recommending new paid tools.
