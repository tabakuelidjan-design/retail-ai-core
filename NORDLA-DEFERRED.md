## Rules

1. Do not implement a deferred item because it sounds useful.
2. Do not remove a deferred item silently.
3. When prerequisites are met, create an explicit implementation decision.
4. A vendor announcement may change priority, not constitutional authority.
5. Emerging systems such as Fujitsu retail agents are benchmarks and evidence sources, not automatic architecture templates.
